# Code of conduct

Woven Matter contributors are expected to make participation safe, respectful,
and productive for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience,
or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the project's security boundaries.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge mistakes.
- Stop a behavior when another participant or a maintainer says it is harmful.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, sustained
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Reporting

Email [support@wovenmatter.com](mailto:support@wovenmatter.com) to report a
conduct concern privately. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical,
and retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is prohibited.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, temporarily
restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant.

This policy applies in project repositories, issues, discussions, events, and
other spaces where someone represents the project.
