# Code of conduct

WovenMatter contributors are expected to make participation safe, respectful,
and productive for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience,
or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, and project security boundaries.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge mistakes.
- Stop behavior when another participant or a maintainer says it is harmful.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, sustained
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security contact. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as practical, and retaliation is prohibited.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, events, and other
spaces where someone represents the project.
